Manage Data Page

This is the page where you manage lists of bills or archive and restore lists of bills, people and venues.

Most fields are obvious, but you can tap on any highlighted area for further explanation (on a desktop browser you can also hover over an area to see its name). This page is unusual in that it is too long for most dvices and you must scroll up to see the end of it.
