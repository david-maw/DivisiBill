# Licenses Page

This is the Licenses page, where you can purchase OCR scans and manage subscriptions, you reach it from the Settings page. For more information about licensing see  [licensing help](licensing.html).

Most fields are obvious, but you can tap on any highlighted area for further explanation (on a desktop browser you can also hover over an area to see its name).
